Sleeve privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Sleeve does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account or sign-in, and there are no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Your ratings, notes, shelves, history and settings are saved only on your device. They leave it only when you choose to export a backup.

When you search or explore music, Sleeve sends your search text directly to Apple's public iTunes Search API and, for pasted Spotify links, to Spotify's oEmbed service. These requests return artwork, track details and previews. Those services handle requests under their own privacy policies. Sleeve does not run any servers of its own.

If you delete the app, your data is deleted with it. Export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
